小板凳隐私说明

更新于 2026-11-24

小板凳用来录下家里长辈讲的故事。这些录音是你家的私事，所以我们的做法很简单：东西都留在你自己的手机和你自己的 iCloud 里，我们看不到，也不收集。

我们不收集什么

没有账号，不需要注册。

没有我们自己的服务器，你的录音、照片、家谱、留言不会传给我们。

没有广告，没有统计分析，没有第三方追踪，不卖也不分享任何数据。

你的东西存在哪里

录音、照片、家谱和文字都存在你的 iPhone 上。

如果你的 iPhone 登录了 iCloud，小板凳会把一份备份放进你自己的 iCloud 云盘，换手机或重装时可以找回。这份备份由 Apple 保管，受你的 Apple 账号保护。

导出和纪念册只在你点了之后生成，由你决定存到哪里、发给谁。

家人留言

你邀请家人后，留言（语音和你填的称呼）通过 Apple 的 iCloud 共享功能，存放在邀请人的 iCloud 里，只有拿到邀请链接并加入的人能看到和听到。

谁拿到链接谁就能加入，所以请只发给家里人。邀请人可以在 iPhone 的 设置 › Apple 账号 › iCloud 里管理共享。

用到的权限

麦克风：录音和录留言。

相机和照片：给每一期和家里人配照片。

这些权限随时可以在 iPhone 的 设置 › 小板凳 里关掉。

删除

在 App 里可以删掉任何一期、任何一位家人。

删掉小板凳 App 会清掉手机上的全部内容；iCloud 里的备份可以在 设置 › Apple 账号 › iCloud › 管理存储 里删除。

儿童

小板凳不面向儿童收集任何信息，也不收集任何人的信息。

联系我们

有任何问题，可以通过 App Store 上小板凳页面里的联系方式找到我们。这份说明有变化时，会在这里更新日期。

Kneehigh Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-11-24

Kneehigh is for recording the stories your elders tell. Those recordings are your family's business, so our approach is simple: everything stays on your own phone and in your own iCloud. We can't see it and we don't collect it.

What we don't collect

There are no accounts and no sign-up.

We run no servers of our own. Your recordings, photos, family tree and messages are never sent to us.

No ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking. We never sell or share any data.

Where your things are kept

Recordings, photos, your family tree and notes are stored on your iPhone.

If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, Kneehigh keeps a backup in your own iCloud Drive so you can get it back on a new phone. Apple holds that backup, protected by your Apple Account.

Exports and keepsake books are only made when you tap for them, and you decide where they go.

Family messages

When you invite family, messages (the voice note and the name you chose) are kept in the inviter's iCloud using Apple's iCloud sharing. Only people who open the invite link and join can see and hear them.

Anyone with the link can join, so only send it to family. The inviter can manage sharing in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud.

Permissions

Microphone: to record stories and messages.

Camera and Photos: to add pictures to recordings and to people in your family tree.

You can turn these off at any time in Settings › Kneehigh.

Deleting

You can delete any recording or person in the app.

Deleting the app removes everything on the phone. The iCloud backup can be deleted in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Children

Kneehigh doesn't collect information from children, or from anyone.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact details on Kneehigh's App Store page. If this policy changes, the date here will change too.
